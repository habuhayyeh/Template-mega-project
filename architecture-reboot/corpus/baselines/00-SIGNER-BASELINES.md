## BASELINE-01 — Numbness

*Fictional teaching case / template.*

**Relevant neurologic history / PMHx**

No prior neuropathy, radiculopathy, stroke, or other known neurologic disease.

## BASELINE-02 — Syncope

*Fictional teaching case / template.*

### SUBJECTIVE

**Reason for consult:** Syncope.

**HPI:** 29F with no known prior neurologic or cardiac disease who presented after a witnessed loss of consciousness at work.

She had been standing for ~20 mins in a warm room when she began feeling lightheaded and nauseated with diaphoresis, muffled hearing, and progressive darkening of her vision. She told a coworker that she felt as though she was going to pass out, then lost consciousness and was assisted to the floor. Per her coworker, she was unresponsive for ~20–30 secs without stiffening or rhythmic shaking. No tongue bite or urinary incontinence. She awakened spontaneously and was immediately able to recognize those around her. She felt tired and mildly nauseated afterward but was not confused.

She had eaten little that morning and reports limited fluid intake throughout the day. No preceding chest pain, palpitations, shortness of breath, severe headache, focal weakness, numbness, diplopia, or vertigo. Denies starting new medications or changes to lifestyle.

**Relevant neurologic history / PMHx**

One prior episode of presyncope during a blood draw several years ago without LOC. No known seizures, stroke, or other neurologic disease.

**Other PMHx:** Iron-deficiency anemia.

**Social history**

Lives with her partner and works as a teacher. No tobacco or recreational drug use. Drinks alcohol socially.

**Medications**

- Ferrous sulfate 325 mg PO QD.

### OBJECTIVE

**Physical examination**

**Vitals:** BP 118/72, HR 76, RR 16, SpO2 99% on room air, T 36.8°C.

**Neurologic:**

- Mental status: Awake, alert, and appropriately interactive. At neurologic baseline.
- Cranial nerves intact.
- Motor: 5/5 throughout.
- Reflexes 2+ and symmetric.
- Sensation intact.
- Coordination without appendicular ataxia.
- Gait normal.

**Labs**

- CBC: Hgb 11.2 g/dL; otherwise without significant abnormality.
- BMP: Na 139 mmol/L, K 4.1 mmol/L, glucose 92 mg/dL; otherwise without significant abnormality.

**Other workup**

- **Orthostatic vitals:** No significant orthostatic BP drop or compensatory tachycardia.
- **ECG:** Normal sinus rhythm without acute ischemic change or conduction abnormality.

### ASSESSMENT AND PLAN

#### // Vasovagal syncope

- Progressive lightheadedness, nausea, diaphoresis, muffled hearing, and darkening vision while standing prior to LOC.
- Witnessed LOC lasting ~20–30 secs without tonic stiffening, rhythmic shaking, tongue bite, or urinary incontinence.
- Immediate return of awareness W/O post-event confusion.
- Limited PO intake and prolonged standing in a warm environment.
- Prior presyncope with blood draw.
- Neurologic examination at baseline W/O focal deficit.
- Orthostatic vitals unrevealing.
- ECG with normal sinus rhythm.

**Plan:**

- No indication for routine EEG.
- No indication for neuroimaging from a neurologic standpoint.
- Encourage adequate hydration and regular meals.
- Avoid recognized triggers when possible and sit or lie down with recurrent presyncopal symptoms.
- Further workup per cardiology/ primary team.
