## Which Handoff was attached — unconfirmed, and it matters

`O54` is absent from `runs/2026-11-14-p003/handoff_v2.txt`, the Handoff generated from `ap_ledger_v4.txt`. So the runs almost certainly used the older `runs/2026-11-14-p002/handoff.txt`, which still contains it.
