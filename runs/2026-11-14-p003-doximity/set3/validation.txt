Two of the three runs print the same infarct twice and the same CT twice, in a note that is supposed to have one fact per bullet. All three print the family concern twice.

## Reading

The rule exists in `05` — *one fact, one bullet* — and it fails exactly where the Handoff carries a fact twice from different sources. That is the case the lossless `04` was introduced to preserve and `05` was supposed to compress. It is now the dominant defect.

`apixaban held` and `Apixaban to resume` splitting is the same failure in the plan: they are one decision with a condition, and only run 3 kept them together.

## Verdict
