## 5. Verdict

**FAIL.** I2, I3, I4, I7, I10 differ across three runs on one input. The rewrite made the output better and still did not make it reproducible.

Two findings beyond the invariants:

1. **D-014 was breached by run 1.** O54 rendered as a seizure-block fact. D-014 says it stays out of the A/P. A stage that can render a fact a decision excludes is a decision-enforcement gap, not a rendering preference.
2. **`Seizure precautions` is missing from all three runs.** It is a `STANDING_ITEMS` entry whose trigger — a seizure as the working diagnosis — is satisfied. Three runs, three omissions: that is not drift, it is the rule not being applied.

## 7. Next

Do not treat `05` as accepted. Three candidate causes, in order of how well the runs support them:

- the COURSE relevance gate is a judgement with no anchor, so `admitted`, `encephalomalacia` and `no new focal lesion` drift;
- `STANDING_ITEMS` is not enforced anywhere, so an item appears or not by chance;
- D-014 has no mechanism — nothing in `05` knows a decided exclusion exists.

The first two are fixable inside `05`. The third is not: it needs the exclusion to live in the input, or a check after the fact.
