# STANDING ITEMS

Plan items that may appear without a source sentence. Closed list.

An item is added only when its trigger is satisfied. If the trigger is not literally satisfied, the item does not appear. Silence is the default, and a missing standing item is never an error.

**Nothing here is matched by a program.** The writer reads the trigger and applies it, so it is a judgment with a conservative default. Where the claim is a comparison and you cannot tell which side is being treated, leave the item out. A missing line costs one sentence; a wrong one puts a statement into a note that gets signed.

A trigger is **not** satisfied by the diagnosis appearing:

- only in `EVIDENCE`
- as the rejected alternative — `focal seizure favored over recurrent ischemia`, where the stroke is what is being argued against
- as one side of an unresolved comparison — `stroke vs seizure remains unresolved`
- negated — `no stroke`
- as a differential still open — `stroke not excluded`, `cannot rule out stroke`

---

## Stroke

**Trigger.** `CLAIM` names ischemic stroke, intracerebral haemorrhage, or subarachnoid haemorrhage as the working diagnosis — plainly, or as the diagnosis favoured over an alternative.

**Item.** Discussed prognosis and provided handoffs regarding the importance of secondary prevention and PT/OT.

Bucket: everything else. Once per note, not once per problem.

---

## Seizure

**Trigger.** `CLAIM` names a seizure or epilepsy as the working diagnosis.

**Item.** Seizure precautions

Bucket: everything else.

---

## Myasthenia gravis

**Trigger.** `CLAIM` names myasthenia gravis, or an exacerbation of it, as the working diagnosis.

**Item.** Review medications that impair neuromuscular transmission

Bucket: everything else.
