1. Problem heading and role.
2. Status.

The current task creates the home for these instructions. Do not treat this guide as an approved executable prompt.

## Common requirements to preserve

- Use only supplied clinical content; retain supporting source references.
- Preserve speaker, attribution, fact time, uncertainty, negation, and causal targets.
- Preserve complete management meaning, including conditions, timing, ownership, patient response, and decision versus execution state.
- Do not infer diagnosis from medication, turn historical status into current status, or adopt outside interpretations without support.
- Retain relevant counterevidence and expose unresolved or unassigned material.
- Distinguish documented meaning from proposed organization.
- Keep reconciliation and architecture instructions identical across the initial extraction comparison.
- Do not introduce new reasoning or standard-of-care additions to complete a worksheet.
- Apply the same output requirements and semantic evaluation criteria to every method.
